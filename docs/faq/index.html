Frequently asked questions

What exactly do I get when I buy?

A structured blueprint delivered as a Google Doc: the full overview, scale path, moat, a concrete first step and the source yield estimate for business blueprints; or the concept, data model, automation plan and UI spec for software tools.

How do I receive it?

Right after payment you get an email with a secure link, and the Doc is shared to the Google account email you enter at checkout. Use a Gmail or Google Workspace address.

Can I preview before paying?

Yes. Every product page has a free preview, and you can browse the whole catalog without signing up.

Are the income figures guaranteed?

No. Revenue figures are unverified illustrative estimates from the source research. Results depend on your execution, market and pricing. This is not financial or professional advice.

How was this content produced?

The blueprints are compiled with AI-assisted research and are meant as structured starting points. Always validate demand locally before investing money.

What is your refund policy?

See our refund page. In short: if your file doesn't open or is materially different from the page description, contact us within 7 days.

Can I resell or share the blueprint?

Your purchase is for your own personal or business use. Reselling or redistributing the document is not permitted.

Who do I contact for help?

Use the contact page. We reply by email.
